Read the numbers carefully

Read from Canada ranks article views originating in Canada, across Wikipedia languages. Country counts are privacy-protected upper bounds, not exact readership or unique people. A missing article is not zero views.

Canadian topics worldwide measures global human/user pageviews for a curated, expandable topic list. A rise compares the chosen day with the preceding 28 days where available. It does not explain the cause.

A growing historical archive. Each successful collection saves compact monthly files. Archived days retain their first observed values and topic selection, even after they leave the recent snapshot. Missing dates remain gaps; older topic dates may have no Canadian-country ranking. Download the archive manifest.

Reporting days use UTC. This is a dated daily snapshot, not a live news feed. Article names and topics are community maintained.
